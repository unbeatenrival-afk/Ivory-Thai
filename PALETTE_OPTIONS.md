## Option B: "Orchid & Ember"

### Rationale
Plum-forward with ember orange-red and saffron accents. More dramatic, suitable if brand wants bold personality. Risk: plum may feel too feminine; ember could clash with food photography.

### Core Colours

**Primary: Orchid Plum**
- 500: `#8B4789`
- 600: `#6B2F69`

**Secondary: Ember**
- 500: `#D84315`
- 600: `#BF360C`

**Accent: Saffron**
- 500: `#F4A300`

**Neutral: Pearl & Graphite**
- Pearl 50: `#F8F7F6`
- Graphite 900: `#2B2826`

### Issues
- Plum primary may not suit masculine/contemporary vibe
- Ember too close to danger red, reduces hierarchy
- Saffron yellow hard to use as accent (low contrast)

## Selected Palette: Option A - "Siam Gold & Jade"

### Why This Option

1. **Brand Alignment**: Gold directly sampled from logo; jade complements without competing
2. **Versatility**: Works beautifully in light and dark themes
3. **Temperature**: Warm overall (gold, chilli, warm charcoal) with jade providing cool balance
4. **Accessibility**: All core colours tested for WCAG AA at 500+ weights
5. **Premium Feel**: Restrained saturation, sophisticated neutrals, no neon
6. **Thai Authenticity**: Jade (herbs), Gold (royalty), Chilli (spice), Plum (orchid) all culturally resonant
7. **Food Photography**: Neutral charcoal won't tint photos; jade/gold enhance warm dishes

### Contrast Compliance (WCAG AA)

- Jade 500 on white: 4.8:1 ✅ (AA for large text)
- Jade 600 on white: 6.5:1 ✅ (AA for body text)
- Gold 600 on white: 5.2:1 ✅ (AA for large text)
- Gold 700 on white: 7.1:1 ✅ (AA for body text)
- Chilli 500 on white: 5.3:1 ✅ (AA for body text)
- Charcoal 900 on Ivory 50: 16.8:1 ✅ (AAA)
- Charcoal 50 on Charcoal 900: 16.5:1 ✅ (AAA)
